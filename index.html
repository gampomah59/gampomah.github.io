Selected publication · 2025

Long-lived animals reveal clues about α-synuclein aggregation

Our comparative study found that α-synuclein sequences from long-lived animals generally aggregate less readily than sequences from shorter-lived animals, including humans.

Ampomah, G.B., Hard, E.R. & Pratt, M.R. ChemBioChem 26, e202500340.
