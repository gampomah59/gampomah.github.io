From natural products to precision protein chemistry

My scientific path began at KNUST, where I studied bioactive natural products, antimicrobial molecules, and analytical chemistry. At USC, I shifted toward the molecular control of proteins—combining synthesis, biochemistry, and cell-based approaches to study post-translational modifications.

Selected publication · 2025

Long-lived animals reveal clues about α-synuclein aggregation

Our comparative study found that α-synuclein sequences from long-lived animals generally aggregate less readily than sequences from shorter-lived animals, including humans.

Ampomah, G.B., Hard, E.R. & Pratt, M.R. ChemBioChem 26, e202500340.
