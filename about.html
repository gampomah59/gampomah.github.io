About

I am a protein chemist and chemical biologist wuth expertise in peptide synthesis, protein semisynthesis, and biochemical assay development. My research focuses on understanding how protein sequence and post-translational modifications regulate protein structure and function. I employ synthetic and recombinant protein chemistry to investigate disease-relevant proteins, including alpha-synuclein and HSP27. I also develop chemical tools, including metabolic chemical reporters, to investigate protein glycosylation in cellular systems. Broadly, my research integrates synthetic chemistry, protein biochemistry, and chemical biology to uncover molecular mechanisms underlying protein regulation and disease.
